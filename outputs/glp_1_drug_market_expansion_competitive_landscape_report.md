## Executive Summary
The top recommendation is to **implement the AML Transaction Monitoring Enhancement** (see Decision Matrix) to close the critical AML compliance gap identified in cross‑border biotech financing. This action is expected to safeguard regulatory standing, protect the bank’s reputation, and preserve the ability to fund high‑growth GLP‑1 biotech deals. The biggest risk remains **Credit Exposure to GLP‑1 Biotech Firms**, a high‑strategic risk that could amplify losses if market dynamics shift abruptly. Addressing AML risk now creates a compliance foundation that enables disciplined credit‑risk management for the expanding GLP‑1 market.
